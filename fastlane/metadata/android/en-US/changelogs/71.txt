Homework text can now be copied — a copy button on every homework card and in the lesson detail. Fixed the current-lesson highlight getting stuck on a lesson that was already over. Fixed weeks getting mixed up when the app was left open across midnight. New themes: Nord, Dracula, Solarized Light and Catppuccin Latte. Website and Report an issue now point at this fork's GitHub page. The combined Homework tab now clearly separates school homework from your own tasks. The next-lesson card is shorter, names the weekday, and its countdown now matches how far away the lesson is instead of always spelling out days, hours, minutes and seconds. The compact next-lesson card is optional — turn it on in Settings → Timetable.
